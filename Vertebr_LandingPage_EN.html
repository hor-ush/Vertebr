Scoliosis is treated with exercises, but at home they are done without supervision, and mistakes go unnoticed.

This is what the device will look like

  • Rings are motion sensors. One on the chest (shoulder-blade level), one on the pelvis (above the sacrum).
  • Green bands are an elastic belt and strap with a front fastening. You put them on yourself.
  • The dark strip along the back is elastic and holds the cable. The dark rectangle at the front is a box with the board, battery and buzzer.

Try it yourself

  1. Turn the sound on.
  2. Press “Show correct position”, then “Make a mistake” to hear the cue.
  3. Move the sliders yourself: the green zone means correct, and outside it you will hear a sound.

Exercise: side-lying. The chest and pelvis are corrected in opposite directions.

Rings in the drawings show where the sensors are placed.

How it works

  1. The therapist sets the green zone: movement limits for your exercise and level.
  2. Two sensors on the belt and chest strap measure the body’s tilts and rotations.
  3. The device stays silent while you are in the zone and sounds a cue when the movement is too small or too large.

What this is not

This is not a brace, a diagnosis or a replacement for a therapist. The device does not measure the curve angle (that is done from an X-ray) and cannot tell whether muscles are tense. It detects sideways shift through tilt. Where and how much to correct depends on the curve, age and treatment stage, and the therapist decides this. The limits in the demo are illustrative.

Data

The device stores only angles during the exercise, and children need parental consent. This page collects nothing. The feedback form is separate, and giving a contact there is voluntary.

Where the project is now

This is a student project. We have surveys of patients, parents and therapists, and consultations with specialists. The device does not exist yet, so for now we are collecting feedback.
